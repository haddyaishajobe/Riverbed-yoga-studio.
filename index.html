Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our mission

Riverbend offers a calm place to move, rest, and reconnect. We welcome beginners, working adults, and older neighbors with supportive instruction and options for different abilities.

New to yoga? You belong here.

You do not need to be flexible or know any poses. Come as you are, take breaks when you need them, and ask about adaptations before class.

Featured class: Gentle Yoga

Explore steady movement, accessible poses, and breathing at a comfortable pace. Read about Gentle Yoga and other classes.

Schedule overview

Gentle, Vinyasa, and Restorative classes are offered as part of our studio program. Exact class times and availability can be requested through the interest form.
