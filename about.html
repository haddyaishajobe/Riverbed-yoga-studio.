Accessibility and accommodations

Students are welcome to request chair-based options, slower transitions, breaks, or guidance for a first visit. Please describe your needs in the workshop interest form so the team can discuss suitable options. Specific building accessibility features should be confirmed with the studio.

Studio policies

  • Arrive a little early to settle in and share questions.
  • Respect other students' space and privacy.
  • Move within your comfort range; resting is always welcome.
  • Ask the studio about cancellations, payments, and accessibility before attending.
