Classes and Services

Group classes

Gentle Yoga

Slow-paced movement and simple breathing practices with options for seated or supported poses. A reassuring starting point for beginners and older adults.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing practice linking movement and breath. Students are encouraged to adjust the pace and rest whenever needed.

Restorative Yoga

Quiet, supported poses and time to settle. A restful option after demanding workdays or busy weeks.
